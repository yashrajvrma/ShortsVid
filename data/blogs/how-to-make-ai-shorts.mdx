## Why AI Shorts Are Dominating in 2025

Short-form video is the fastest growing content format on the internet. YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok collectively serve **over 2 billion users** daily. The problem? Creating quality content consistently is hard.

That's where AI changes everything.

## Step 1 — Pick the Right Topic

Not all topics perform equally. Here are the highest-performing categories:

- **Horror Stories** — massive engagement, high watch time
- **Motivational** — shareable, evergreen
- **History Facts** — educational, broad audience
- **Life Hacks** — high rewatch value

> Pro tip: Start with Horror Stories if you want fast growth. They consistently outperform other niches in the first 30 days.

## Step 2 — Write a Great Script

Your script is everything. Keep it:

1. **Under 60 seconds** when read aloud
2. **Hook in the first 3 words** — no slow starts
3. **One clear idea** — don't try to cover too much

## Step 3 — Choose Your Video Style

ShortsVid supports 7 video styles. Here's when to use each:

| Style           | Best For              |
| --------------- | --------------------- |
| Photo Realistic | Horror, Documentary   |
| Anime           | Storytelling, Fantasy |
| Cyberpunk       | Tech, Mystery         |
| Cinematic       | Motivational, Drama   |

## Step 4 — Pick Your Voice

Voice is 50% of the experience. A mismatch between voice and content kills retention. For horror — use a deep, slow voice. For motivational — use high energy.

## Final Thoughts

The barrier to quality content creation is gone. With the right tools and workflow, you can produce **7 shorts per week** in under an hour.

Start with one. Test it. Double down on what works.
